🌙 Eid ul-Fitr Sunnah

1.Eat dates before going for the Eid prayer (preferably in odd numbers).
2.Perform Ghusl (take a bath) and maintain cleanliness before the prayer.
3.Wear your best and clean clothes on Eid day.
4.Go early to the Eid prayer and pray in congregation.
5.Recite the Takbir (“Allahu Akbar…”) on the way to the prayer.
6.Use one route to go and a different route to return from the prayer.
7.Give Zakat al-Fitr before the Eid prayer to help the poor.
8.Do not fast on the day of Eid and celebrate with happiness.
